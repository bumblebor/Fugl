How we calculate cost

FUGL (Flights Under Guided Lookup) calculates the fair price of your flights based off of
the current day's fuel cost for planes and the distance between airports. We use
up-to-date information about fuel price to determine the fairest price. FUGL
does not always predict exact prices, but rather aims to give a fair price for your booking.
As a result, prices may fluctuate above or below the price listed here.
